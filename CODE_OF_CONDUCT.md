# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Lumiere Pro to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent and ask for clarification before assuming otherwise.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people for whom English is a second language.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, personal or political attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Public or private harassment, stalking, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a violation privately, use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and title it
"Conduct report" (it reaches maintainers only), or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers who are the subject of a report are recused from handling it.
